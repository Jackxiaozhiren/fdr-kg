# Historical KBS-era materials (superseded)

The folders in this directory are the **superseded Knowledge-Based Systems (KBS) era** manuscript and
submission package, dated 2026-07-26. They are retained for audit and version history only.

**Important:** These materials contain **outdated numbers, claims, and figures** (including FB15k-237
figures and a "TransE leads discoveries" narrative) that were subsequently corrected and are **not** part of
the accepted ESWA submission. They also contain the earlier KBS manuscript text that triggered a text-similarity
rejection. Do **not** use them to reproduce or represent the current submission.

The current submission is the manuscript "Reliable Knowledge Discovery in Knowledge Graphs: A Statistical
Quality Assurance Framework for Link Prediction", submitted to Expert Systems with Applications; its accepted
quantitative evidence is the frozen, hash-verified WN18RR record (see the top-level README).
