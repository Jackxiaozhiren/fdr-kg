# Highlights

- KG link prediction reformulated as large-scale multiple hypothesis testing
- Empirical p-values from embedding scores enable per-prediction FDR control
- 26--85% of test triples are statistically indistinguishable from noise
- Per-relation FDR rates span 9.1%--96.5%, exposing heterogeneity invisible to MRR
- MRR and FDR produce conflicting model rankings across both benchmark datasets
