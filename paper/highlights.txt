# Highlights

- Knowledge graph link prediction evaluation reformulated as statistical quality assurance for knowledge-based systems
- Empirical p-values from embedding scores enable per-prediction false discovery rate control
- 26--85% of test triples are statistically indistinguishable from noise across models and benchmarks
- Per-relation discovery rates span from below 10% to above 95%, exposing extreme heterogeneity in prediction reliability
- MRR and FDR capture distinct dimensions of model quality, producing complementary rankings
